# Reorganisation plan 2027

From 1 January 2027, planning and dispatch merge into one Operations department led by the head of operations. Nobody loses their job in this change.

The Rotterdam planning desk moves to the Amsterdam office by April 2027. Staff in Rotterdam can choose to move, to work hybrid with travel reimbursed, or to take a voluntary leaving package.

Engineering gets a dedicated platform team of four, formed from the current team, responsible for the route-planning system.

Timeline: announcement to all staff in November 2026, individual conversations in December, new structure live on 1 January 2027.
