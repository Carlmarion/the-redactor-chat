# Hiring process

1. The manager writes the vacancy with HR, including the salary band.
2. HR publishes the vacancy for two weeks internally, then externally.
3. First interview: manager and one team member, 45 minutes.
4. Second interview: a practical case. Engineers get a take-home exercise of at most four hours; planners do a live planning case.
5. Reference check: two references, done by HR, only with the candidate's consent.
6. Offer: HR sends the offer within three working days of the final interview.

Every candidate gets an answer, also when rejected. Interview notes are deleted four weeks after the vacancy closes, unless the candidate agrees to stay in our talent pool for one year.
