# On-call rota and escalation

One engineer is on call each week, Monday 09:00 to Monday 09:00. The rota is in the engineering calendar. Swaps are allowed if both engineers agree and update the calendar.

On-call pay is €250 per week plus €40 per hour of incident work outside office hours.

Escalation: if the on-call engineer cannot fix a production issue within 30 minutes, they call the lead engineer. If route planning is down for more than one hour, the head of operations is informed so planners can switch to the paper fallback.
