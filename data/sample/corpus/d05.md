# Salary bands 2026

Confidential. For payroll and HR use only.

| Band | Role examples | Annual gross range |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Junior planner, warehouse lead | €34,000 – €41,000 |
| B2 | Planner, engineer | €42,000 – €55,000 |
| B3 | Senior engineer, senior planner | €56,000 – €72,000 |
| B4 | Lead engineer, team manager | €73,000 – €90,000 |
| B5 | Head of department | €91,000 – €120,000 |

Bands are reviewed every January against the transport and logistics collective agreement. A move between bands requires approval from the head of department and HR.
