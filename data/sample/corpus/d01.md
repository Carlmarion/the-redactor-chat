# Welcome to Noordlicht

Noordlicht Logistics moves temperature-controlled freight between the Port of Amsterdam, Rotterdam and distribution centres across the Benelux. We are 140 people: drivers, planners, engineers and the office team on the Houthavens.

Your first week: you get a laptop and an access badge from IT on day one. Your manager sets up a buddy who answers the questions you feel silly asking. On Thursday there is a welcome lunch for everyone who started that month.

Where to find things: company policies live in the shared drive under Public/handbook. Office rules for the Amsterdam building are in Public/kantoor. If you cannot find a document, ask your manager rather than a colleague in another team; access to some folders is restricted on purpose.

Who to ask: HR questions go to hr@noordlicht.example. IT problems go to the helpdesk at extension 4400. Anything about your pay goes to the payroll team, not to your manager.
