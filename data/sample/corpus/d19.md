# Relocation support

New hires moving to the Amsterdam area from abroad get relocation support.

- A one-off relocation allowance of €3,000 gross, paid with the first salary.
- Up to one month of temporary housing, booked by HR.
- Help applying for the 30% tax ruling, if the hire qualifies.
- A registration appointment at the municipality, arranged by HR in the first week.

Staff who leave within one year of starting repay half of the relocation allowance.
