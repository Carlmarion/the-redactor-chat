# Bonus scheme

The bonus pool is 6% of the operating profit of the previous year. It is shared across staff in bands B2 to B5.

Each person's bonus is a share of the pool based on their band and a performance rating from 1 to 4. A rating of 1 gives no bonus. A rating of 4 doubles the band share.

Bonuses are paid with the March salary. Staff who leave before 1 March do not receive a bonus for the previous year.
