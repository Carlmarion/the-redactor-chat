# Employee handbook (HR edition)

This edition is for the HR team. It adds the working notes HR uses when applying the public policies. Staff-facing versions of each policy live in Public/handbook.

## 1. Onboarding checklist

Before day one, HR sends the contract for e-signature, registers the new hire with the pension fund, and asks IT for a laptop and badge. On day one, HR checks the identity document in person, as Dutch law requires before the first working day, and stores a copy in the personnel file for five years after the contract ends.

During the first month, HR schedules a check-in at week two and week four. The buddy is chosen by the manager, but HR makes sure nobody is a buddy to more than one new hire at a time.

## 2. Probation period

Permanent contracts have a probation period of two months. Fixed-term contracts of more than six months have one month. A contract of six months or less has no probation period. Ending a contract during probation needs a short written reason, even though the law does not require one, so that we can answer questions from the employee later.

## 3. Contract types

Most new hires start on a one-year fixed-term contract. After two fixed-term contracts, or after three years in total, the next contract is permanent by law. HR tracks these dates in the HR portal and warns the manager three months before a fixed-term contract ends.

## 4. Absence handling

When someone reports sick for more than three days in a row, HR contacts them on day four to ask how they are and whether they need anything. HR never asks about the medical cause; only the company doctor may discuss that. After six weeks, HR and the manager write a problem analysis with the company doctor, then a plan of approach within eight weeks. These are legal deadlines under the Gatekeeper Improvement Act.

## 5. Salary and compensation

Salaries follow the bands in the payroll folder. When making an offer, HR may offer up to the midpoint of the band without further approval. Above the midpoint needs the head of department. A senior engineer offer in band B3 can therefore go up to €64,000 without escalation.

Annual raises are decided in January. The default increase is the collective agreement index, currently 3.1%, plus up to 4% for performance. The total pay budget for raises in 2026 is €410,000 across the company; payroll holds the per-person breakdown.

Bonuses follow the bonus scheme in the payroll folder and are paid in March.

## 6. Leaving the company

The notice period is one month for the employee and depends on length of service for the employer. HR holds an exit conversation in the last two weeks and asks IT to close accounts on the last working day at 17:00. The final salary includes payment for unused leave days.

## 7. Confidentiality

Personnel files, salary data and medical information are never shared outside HR and payroll. If a manager asks for a team member's salary, HR shares only the band, not the exact amount, unless the head of department approves.
