# Payroll calendar 2026

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. When the 25th falls on a weekend, salary is paid on the Friday before.

December is an exception: salaries are paid on 18 December so staff have their pay before the holidays. The holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) of 8% is paid with the May salary.

Payroll cut-off for changes such as new hires, leavers and overtime is the 12th of the month. Changes after the 12th go into the next month's run.
