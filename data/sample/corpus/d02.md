# Leave policy

Applies to all permanent staff from 1 January 2026.

## Annual leave

Full-time staff get 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, on top of Dutch public holidays. Part-time staff get leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave is booked in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for periods longer than three days.

## Carrying over

Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and must be taken before 1 July. Days beyond that expire, unless your manager agreed in writing that business needs prevented you from taking them.

## Sick leave

Report sick to your manager before 09:30 on the first day. After two weeks of sickness the company doctor (bedrijfsarts) gets involved, as Dutch law requires.

## Special leave

You get 2 days for moving house, 1 day for your own wedding, and up to 5 days of bereavement leave for a close family member.
