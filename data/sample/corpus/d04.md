# Remote work

Office staff may work from home up to three days a week. Tuesday is the shared office day for every team, so meetings with several teams are planned then.

You need a stable connection and a quiet place to take calls. Noordlicht pays a home-working allowance of €2.40 per day worked from home, paid monthly.

Working from abroad is allowed for up to 20 days a year, inside the EU only, and must be registered with HR beforehand for tax reasons.

Planners and drivers work on site; this policy does not apply to operational roles.
