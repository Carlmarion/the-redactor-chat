# Budget 2027 (draft)

Draft for discussion in the finance team and management. Figures are not final.

Total operating budget: €18.4 million, up 6% on 2026.

- Fleet and fuel: €9.1 million. Includes four new electric trucks for the Amsterdam city routes.
- Staff: €6.2 million, assuming 8 new hires.
- IT and software: €1.3 million, including the new route-planning system.
- Marketing: €420,000, mostly trade fairs in Rotterdam and Antwerp.
- Office and facilities: €780,000.
- Contingency: €600,000.

Open point: the electricity price for charging is uncertain; finance will update the fleet line in November.
