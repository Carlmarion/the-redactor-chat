# Deployment guide

All services deploy through the pipeline; nobody deploys from a laptop.

1. Open a pull request. Two approvals are needed for the route-planning service, one for everything else.
2. Merge to main. The pipeline builds, runs tests and deploys to staging.
3. Check staging. Promote to production from the pipeline with the "promote" button.

No production deploys on Friday after 14:00, or on the day before a public holiday, unless it fixes an incident.

Rollback: press "rollback" on the previous release in the pipeline. It takes about two minutes.
