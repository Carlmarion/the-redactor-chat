# Password policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Use a passphrase, not a word with numbers added.

Use the company password manager for every work account. Never reuse a work password anywhere else.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the finance portal and every production system.

If you think a password has leaked, change it immediately and tell the helpdesk at extension 4400. You will not get in trouble for reporting.
