# Expense claims

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

Travel: second-class train is the default for trips in the Netherlands and Belgium. Taxis are reimbursed only between 23:00 and 06:00 or when carrying equipment. Car travel is reimbursed at €0.23 per kilometre.

Meals: when travelling for work, up to €15 for lunch and €35 for dinner, alcohol excluded.

Equipment: home-office purchases above €150 need your manager's approval before you buy.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.
