# Incident report: September outage

On 17 September the route-planning service was down from 06:10 to 08:45. Planners switched to the paper fallback at 06:40.

Cause: a database migration locked the routes table during the morning peak. The migration had been tested on a small copy of the data only.

Actions: migrations now run outside 05:00–10:00, and every migration is tested on a full-size copy first. The on-call escalation was followed correctly.
